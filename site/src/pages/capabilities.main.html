Printed in house, on sixteen heads.

Our digital printing plant runs 16 Kyocera heads, and the steaming, coating and washing are in house as well.

It suits what we do best: bohemian-printed apparel for women and kids.

1 L of water per metre of digitally printed fabric: the designed consumption of the plant.

  • 16

    Kyocera heads on a high-speed digital printer from Atexco.

  • Steam

    A PLC-based loop ager with moist and superheated steaming (Tex-Fab).

  • Coat

    A two-chamber, 10 ft pin and clip coating machine (Stanford).

  • Wash

    A PLC-controlled, five-chamber fabric washing range (Tex-Fab), completed by a two-bowl squeezing mangle and a four-cylinder drying range.

Embroidery, by hand and by machine.

Our hand embroidery and adda work come from our family’s village. The machines on our floor extend them.

By hand

Hand embroidery and adda work, rooted in our family’s village of Modi Nagar and Murad Nagar. Chikan and hand-block print come from the same village craft.

On 24 heads

Computerised embroidery with built-in laser cutting, dori and chikan work, and a 2-sequin, 4-bead system.

Laser and hot-fix

Two fully automatic laser cutters with camera and conveyor, on 1600 × 1900 mm beds, and a computerised hot-fix machine for studs and rhinestones.

Dye, wash and smock

Building quality, not checking it.

Quality is a culture here, not a final inspection. Production is stage-gated: each step is assessed before the next one begins.

Audited, and lighter on the land.

Sedex SMETA 4-pillar audited factory

The four pillars are labour standards, health and safety, business ethics and the environment. Fibres we use include Better Cotton, Lenzing EcoVero, LIVAECO and Lenzing Modal.

  • 95 kWp

    Tata rooftop solar plant, meeting about 30% of the factory’s electricity.

  • 60 m³ a day

    Effluent treatment plant. An ultrafiltration and reverse osmosis upgrade is planned so treated water can be reused.
