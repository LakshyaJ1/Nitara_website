In the 1970s, our father established a state-of-the-art garment manufacturing facility, earning a reputation for quality production.

Our ancestral village in the Murad Nagar–Modi Nagar region represents another part of our heritage: a rich tradition of hand embroidery and intricate handwork.

Since 2007, we have brought these two strengths together at Nitara India. Combining manufacturing expertise with artisan craftsmanship, we continue to introduce fresh design thinking, new technologies and expanded capabilities, creating distinctive collections for international fashion brands while building on the strengths we inherited.

Established & evolving

Established in 2007. Evolving towards a more sustainable future.

After nearly two decades, Couture A Craft marks Nitara India’s next chapter: more sustainable, vertically integrated manufacturing, with greater in-house control over quality and confidentiality.
