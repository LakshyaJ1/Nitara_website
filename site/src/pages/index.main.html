A Legacy of Quality.
A Tradition of Craft.

In the 1970s, our father established a state-of-the-art garment manufacturing facility, earning a reputation for quality production.

Our ancestral village in the Murad Nagar–Modi Nagar region represents another part of our heritage: a rich tradition of hand embroidery and intricate handwork.

Since 2007, we have brought these two strengths together at Nitara India. Combining manufacturing expertise with artisan craftsmanship, we continue to introduce fresh design thinking, new technologies and expanded capabilities, creating distinctive collections for international fashion brands while building on the strengths we inherited.

  1. 1970s The factory

    Our father’s garment manufacturing facility earns its name for quality production.

  2. 2007 Nitara India

    Nitara India brings manufacturing and village handwork together. A design studio, CAD and in-house print follow.

  3. Today Brands worldwide

    Evolving design, printing, embroidery and manufacturing capabilities serve fashion brands worldwide.

    Delicate chikankari and textured cord embroidery, combining traditional artistry with skilled handwork and advanced computerised techniques.

    Intricate embroidery and delicate eyelet patterns create airy textures, decorative borders and beautifully detailed fabrics.

    Intricate chemical embroidery and automated, camera-guided laser cutting create delicate openwork, precise detailing and elegant decorative finishes.

Quality in Every Detail.
Confidence in Every Shipment.

From design development and laboratory testing to final packing, our quality process follows your approved samples and specifications.

  1. Design & Sample Approval

    Design, fit, fabrics, prints and embroidery reviewed against your brief, with sample approval before bulk production.

  2. Material Inspection & Lab Testing

    Fabric and trims inspected for quality, supported by laboratory checks for shrinkage, colourfastness and rubbing fastness.

  3. Print & Embroidery

    Colours, placement, print clarity and embroidery workmanship checked against approved samples.

  4. Cutting & Stitching

    Pattern accuracy, measurements, seam quality and garment construction checked during production.

  5. Washing & Finishing

    Appearance, dimensional stability, shade consistency, cleanliness and pressing checked for a carefully finished garment.

  6. Final Inspection & Packing

    Workmanship, measurements, labels, quantities and packaging reviewed against order requirements before dispatch.
