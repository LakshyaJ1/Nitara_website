Rooted in craft since the 1980s.

Our father started the workshop in the 1980s, on the hand-embroidery and adda craft of our village, Modi Nagar–Murad Nagar.

Today a second generation of NIFT alumni runs Nitara India. We design and make casual, hand-embroidered and bohemian-printed women’s and kids’ wear for some of the world’s premier brands.

  1. 1980s The workshop

    A family workshop built on village hand embroidery.

  2. 2007 Nitara India

    Nitara India Pvt Ltd is founded. CAD, a design studio and in-house print follow.

  3. Today One roof, many labels

    Design, print, embroidery and production for global labels.

Our greatest achievement isn’t our expertise. It’s the bond we share with our customers, our workforce and our community.

Made by hand.
Finished by machine.

Hand embroidery and adda work from our village, with computerised embroidery, digital print and laser cutting on our floor.

  • Hand embroidery & adda

    Florals worked by hand on the adda frame, the way our village has always done it.

  • Chikan & dori

    Shadow-work and cord embroidery, by hand and on 24 computerised heads.

  • Digital print

    Botanicals printed in-house on sixteen Kyocera heads, then steamed and coated.

  • Schiffli & eyelet

    Openwork borders for white-on-white summer dresses.

  • Tie & dye

    Traditional and modern resist techniques, with washes that keep colour bright.

  • Block-print motifs

    Old block patterns re-drawn for bohemian tiers and borders.

  • Cordwork

    Raised cord tracing paisley and leaf.

  • Dip & ombré

    Gradients from deep indigo to sand, dyed by hand.

  • Cutwork

    Openwork hems, cut and finished by hand.

Quality is built in, not checked at the end.

Every garment clears a check at each stage before it moves on.

  1. Receiving

    Fabric, trims and raw material checked on arrival: dimensional stability, colourfastness, shrinkage, even dyeing.

  2. Cutting

    Inspected and spread before the first cut.

  3. Stitching

    Checked in-line, on computerised UVT machines.

  4. Finishing

    Washing, pressing and shade checks under the colour cabinet.

  5. Packing

    Final audit before it leaves the floor.

Responsible by design.

An audited factory, certified fibres, and a footprint we measure.

  • 95 kWp rooftop solar by Tata Solar, meeting about 30% of the factory’s electricity.

  • 1 litre of water per metre of digitally printed fabric: the design rate of our five-chamber washing range.

  • 60 m³ a day through our effluent treatment plant. Ultrafiltration and RO reuse is planned next.

  • Cleaner steam from a pellet-fired boiler with water scrubber, bag filter and online emission monitoring.
