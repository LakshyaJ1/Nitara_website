Printed in house, start to finish.

Our digital printing plant runs on Kyocera print heads, and the steaming, coating and washing are in house as well.

It suits what we do best: bohemian-printed apparel for women and kids.

1 L of water per metre of digitally printed fabric: the designed consumption of the plant.

  • Print

    High-speed digital printing on Kyocera heads, on an Atexco printer.

  • Steam

    A PLC-based loop ager with moist and superheated steaming (Tex-Fab), fixing every print.

  • Coat

    A two-chamber, 10 ft pin and clip coating machine (Stanford).

  • Wash

    A fully automatic, PLC-based, multi-range continuous fabric washing range (Tex-Fab), completed by a squeezing mangle and a cylinder drying range.

Embroidery, by hand and by machine.

Our hand embroidery and adda work come from our family’s village. The machines on our floor extend them.

By hand

Hand embroidery and adda work, rooted in our family’s village of Modi Nagar and Murad Nagar. Chikankari and hand-block motifs come from the same village craft.

By computerised machine

Multi-head computerised embroidery with built-in laser cutting, dori and chikan work, and a sequin and bead system.

Chemical embroidery, laser and hot-fix

Chemical embroidery for delicate openwork. Fully automatic laser cutters with camera and conveyor on large-format beds, and a computerised hot-fix machine for studs and rhinestones.

Dye, wash and smock

Quality Control at Every Stage.

Quality is built in and checked throughout. From incoming fabrics to final packing, checks at each production stage support consistent workmanship, accurate finishing and dependable delivery.

Fabric checks

  • Dimensional stability
  • Colourfastness
  • Controlled shrinkage
  • Uniform dyeing
  • CrockingCrocking meter
  • Colour matchingColour matching cabinet and grey scale cards
  • Fabric inspectionInspection and folding machine

assessed in house, from the day fabric arrives

  1. Fabric & Trim Inspection

    Fabric and trims inspected on arrival, with lab checks for shrinkage, colourfastness and rubbing fastness.

  2. Pattern & Fit Checks

    CAD patterns and fit reviewed against your measurements and brief.

  3. Print & Embroidery Checks

    Colour, placement, print clarity and embroidery workmanship against the approved standard.

  4. Sample Approval

    Nothing goes into bulk until you approve the sample.

  5. In-Line Production Checks

    Cutting accuracy, seams and construction checked while the order is on the line.

  6. Finishing & Measurement Checks

    Shade, dimensional stability, pressing and measurements after washing and finishing.

  7. Final Quality Inspection

    Workmanship, labels and quantities reviewed against the order.

  8. Dispatch Approval

    Packing and paperwork signed off before the shipment leaves.

Audited, and lighter on the land.

Sedex SMETA 4-pillar audited factory

The four pillars are labour standards, health and safety, business ethics and the environment. Fibres we use include Better Cotton, Lenzing EcoVero, LIVAECO and Lenzing Modal, alongside FSC.

In progress

GOTS & GRS: certifications in progress

We are working towards Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) and Global Recycled Standard (GRS) certification, furthering our commitment to responsible sourcing and textile production.

  • 95 kWp

    Rooftop solar: investment in renewable electricity for our manufacturing operations.

  • 60 m³ a day

    Effluent treatment: dedicated treatment capacity for wastewater from production.

  • 5-chamber

    Water-conscious processing: a PLC-controlled washing system designed for efficient water use.

  • Non-fossil

    Steam generated using non-fossil fuel, supported by a water scrubber, bag filtration and online emission monitoring.
