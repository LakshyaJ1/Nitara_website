## Direction contract

THESIS: nitara as a sampling room. Every chapter is a hang tag: story on the front, buyer facts on the back. Refuses the white, serif, single-campaign-photo luxury-label arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: chapter grounds dyed like baths: indigo #1B2440, madder #A42E28, turmeric #DFA12C, undyed manila #D6AC67. Chalk ink #F3EDE3. Tags on cotton string with brass eyelets; running-stitch rules; rubber-stamped codes. Rozha One display, Hanken Grotesk text, Courier Prime codes.

STORY: craft, family, studio, floor, conscience, collection. The visitor believes nitara is a design-led maker with real capacity, then enquires.

FIRST VIEWPORT: full-bleed embroidery macro. The nitara wordmark stitches itself in thread at ~45vw, centre-left. A 3D manila tag swings from a string at top-right carrying the tagline and a stamped "Work with us". Top nav with a "For buyers" tag; tag-counter bottom-right.
