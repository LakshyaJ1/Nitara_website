### Technology Gears
Nitara sturdily trusts that use of the latest technology gears is the essence of growth of any trade entity. Each person working at Nitara has access to the latest computer hardware and licenced software and IT tools. Nitara uses Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) software for various levels of supply chain management which helps a great deal in streamlining procurement, production planning, quality control, logistics, human resource management and financial planning. It seeks to increase the transaction speed by exchanging data in real-time, reduce inventory and increased sales volume by fulfilling customer requirements more efficiently & effectively.

### Quality Standards — Better solutions with Nitara
Our products are subject to intense in-house scrutiny at all levels of procurement and production. All our products are subject to in-house scrutiny for multiple times.

It begins with the receiving of raw materials, trims, fabric etc to every stage of production, vis-a-vis, cutting, stitching, finishing and packing, thus ensuring that the right quality of product reaches the client.

Our fabrics are made to high quality standards with proper dimensional stability, color fastness, shrinkage control and uniform dyeing. Each product is subjected to strict quality control measures to ensure that only the best in terms of quality and design reaches the market. Once cleared, the raw materials are adopted for manufacturing.

The manufacturing process is a very closely watched one. The process is divided into several halves for the purpose of better monitoring. The products matching the quality criteria in a given stage of development graduate to the succeeding stage. Thus we ensure minimization in possibility of lapses in quality surveillance process.
